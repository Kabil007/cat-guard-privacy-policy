Privacy Policy for Cat Guard – Merge Defense

Last updated: October 7, 2026

F11 Software Lab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Cat Guard – Merge Defense. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Cat Guard – Merge Defense does not require you to create an account to play the game.

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, including campaign progress, tutorial completion, and preferences such as music, sound effects, and vibration settings.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, home address, contacts, photos, or private messages through the game.

2. Advertising

Cat Guard – Merge Defense uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

Free users may see interstitial advertisements at appropriate transitions in the game. The game also offers optional rewarded advertisements that players may choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits.

Pro subscribers do not receive interstitial advertisements. Optional rewarded advertisements remain available to Pro subscribers when they choose to use them.

Cat Guard – Merge Defense does not use banner advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information required to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. Depending on the user's device, settings, location, and applicable law, this may include device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, interaction information, and approximate location derived from network information.

Advertising and consent choices may vary depending on your region and applicable privacy laws. Where required, Google's consent and privacy tools may be presented before advertising services are used.

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Players choose whether to watch them.

Rewarded advertisements may provide gameplay benefits such as additional Battle Coins or an opportunity to continue a failed wave. Choosing not to watch a rewarded advertisement does not prevent access to the normal game.

4. Pro Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Cat Guard – Merge Defense may offer Monthly Pro and Annual Pro subscriptions through Google Play.

Payments and subscription transactions are processed by Google Play. F11 Software Lab does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

The game may receive information from Google Play necessary to determine whether a subscription entitlement is active and to provide the purchased Pro features.

Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Google Play and the user's Google Play account.

5. Local Game Data

The game stores certain information locally on your device so that gameplay can function correctly. This may include:

  • Campaign progress
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Music preference
  • Sound-effects preference
  • Vibration preference

This local gameplay data is used to remember your progress and preferences.

6. Third-Party Services

Cat Guard – Merge Defense uses or may use third-party services required for advertising, purchases, distribution, and operation of the game, including:

  • Google Play
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform, where applicable

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Their data practices are controlled by the respective service providers.

7. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the game or its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the game
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences
  • Provide advertising
  • Provide optional rewarded advertisements
  • Process and verify Pro subscriptions
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve stability and performance
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers such as Google where necessary to provide advertising, purchases, app distribution, security, fraud prevention, or other functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Cat Guard – Merge Defense.

Third-party services used by the game may process limited information as described in their own privacy policies and according to applicable laws, platform policies, account settings, and consent requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no electronic storage, transmission, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Local gameplay information remains on your device until it is reset, cleared, removed with application data, or otherwise affected by the device or operating system.

Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their respective policies and legal requirements.

12. Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may have options to manage advertising privacy choices, device advertising settings, and Google Play subscription settings.

Rewarded advertisements are voluntary and are shown only when you choose to request the associated gameplay reward.
